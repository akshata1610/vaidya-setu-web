## 00:00 After the video call

Doctor Rao ends a video consultation with Meera. She’s already explained the medicines out loud. Now she has to type the same directions into a prescription.

## 00:12 Meera at home

Later, Meera reads the prescription at home. She can see the medicine name, but she’s unsure when to take it. She’d rather hear the instructions in Kannada.

## 00:24 From conversation to clarity

Vaidya Setu turns the consultation into a draft the doctor can review. It can also prepare a voice explanation the patient can replay. Here’s how it works.

## 00:36 Sign in and connect your Sarvam key

Open Vaidya Setu and sign in with ChatGPT. Add your Sarvam API key and continue to the consultation. You can also explore a demo as a guest.

## 00:48 Add the conversation

First, add the conversation. You can upload a recording or go to the transcript page. I’ll paste the conversation here and select Generate medication draft.

## 01:00 Review the medication draft

Next, check the draft against what the doctor said. Doctor Rao said morning and night. I’ll change how often to twice daily, then confirm I checked the instructions against the conversation.

## 01:14 Approve the prescription

Select Approve and prepare prescription. The reviewed prescription is ready to print or save as a PDF for the clinician to complete and sign.

## 01:24 Prepare voice instructions

If the patient would like audio, open Voice instructions for the patient. For Meera, I’ll choose Kannada. Prepare the script, check the translation, and approve it after a bilingual review. Then generate the audio, listen to it, and record your review before downloading.

## 01:44 Meera’s Kannada voice instructions

English captions for the sample Kannada audio

DemoMed Alpha, 10 milligrams. Take one tablet in the morning and one at night, after food. Keep taking it this way for three days.

## 01:56 Replay at home

Now Meera can replay the doctor’s directions when she needs them. And Doctor Rao can start with the conversation she’s already had. Vaidya Setu. From conversation to clarity.
